FAQ

Common questions.

If you're not sure how something works, the answer is probably here.

What exactly is SummerClean? ▾
SummerClean is a simple web app that helps holiday rental hosts manage their cleaning schedule. You import your bookings from Airbnb or Booking.com, and the app organises them by apartment and checkout date. Your cleaner gets a separate, simplified view via a private link — they don't need an account or any technical knowledge.
Does my cleaner need to create an account? ▾
No. Your cleaner never needs an email address, a password, or an account of any kind. You share a unique link with them (via WhatsApp, for example), and they access it using a simple 4-digit PIN that you set in Settings. They bookmark the link and use it from then on.
My cleaner is not good with technology. Will they manage? ▾
Yes — this is exactly who SummerClean is designed for. The cleaner view has large text, clear dates, and a single button to mark a cleaning as done. There are no menus, no settings, no confusing screens. Hosts consistently report that their cleaners figured it out in under two minutes without any explanation.
How to import bookings from Airbnb ▾
Step 1 — Export from Airbnb:
  • 1 Go to airbnb.com and log in to your host account
  • 2 Click your profile photo → Switch to hosting
  • 3 Go to Reservations in the top menu
  • 4 Click Export to CSV (top-right corner)
  • 5 Save the file to your device
Step 2 — Import into SummerClean:
  • 6 Open SummerClean and go to the Import screen
  • 7 Select Airbnb as the source
  • 8 Upload the CSV file — bookings appear instantly
How to import bookings from Booking.com ▾
Step 1 — Export from Booking.com:
  • 1 Log in to your Booking.com extranet account
  • 2 Go to Reservations in the top navigation
  • 3 Set the date range for the reservations you want
  • 4 Click Download reservations → choose CSV or Excel
  • 5 Save the file to your device
Step 2 — Import into SummerClean:
  • 6 Open SummerClean and go to the Import screen
  • 7 Select Booking.com as the source
  • 8 Upload the file — bookings appear instantly
How to add direct bookings ▾
Direct bookings (from your own website, phone calls, or other channels) are added manually in SummerClean:
  • 1 Tap the + button (bottom-right of the app)
  • 2 Select your apartment and set the source to Direct
  • 3 Enter the check-in date, check-out date, and number of guests
  • 4 Add any notes for your cleaner (optional)
  • 5 Tap Save — the booking appears immediately

If the same guest also appears on a platform, the platform booking takes priority automatically — no duplicates.

Can I manage more than one apartment? ▾
Yes. There's no limit on apartments. Each property gets its own colour and short name, and you can filter the booking list or calendar by individual apartment. The booking count per property is always visible in the filter bar.
What is the "cleaned" status and how does it work? ▾
Each booking has a cleaned / not cleaned toggle. Your cleaner (or you) taps it once the apartment is ready for the next guest. On your admin view, the daily status banner turns green the moment every today checkout has been marked as cleaned — so you know at a glance whether everything is done, even if you're not physically there.
What does "paid / unpaid" mean? ▾
Paid / unpaid is for you, not for the guest. It tracks whether you have paid your cleaner for that particular cleaning job. When a cleaning is marked as done, you can flip the toggle to "paid" once you've settled up with them. It's a simple way to keep track without a separate spreadsheet.
Is it really free? What's the catch? ▾
It is genuinely free. SummerClean was built by a host who needed it for their own properties. There's no freemium model, no locked features, no trial period, and no credit card required. The full product is available to everyone at no cost. Future optional features (like WhatsApp automation) may be paid add-ons, but everything that exists today will remain free.
Does it work on a phone? Do I need to install anything? ▾
SummerClean is a web app — it opens in any browser on any device. Nothing to install. It is designed specifically for phones: large buttons, simple navigation, and a layout that works on small screens. Your cleaner can bookmark the link on their phone's home screen and it behaves like a normal app.
My cleaner doesn't speak English. Is that a problem? ▾
Not at all. SummerClean is available in English, French and Portuguese. Language can be switched in one tap from anywhere in the app — both the admin view and the cleaner view support all three languages.
