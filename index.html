Questions

Frequently asked.

Is there a free plan?
Yes — the Starter plan is free for up to 2 properties, no credit card required. For more properties or premium features, Pro is €7/month.
Do I need to install an app?
No. SummerFlow runs in your browser on any device — phone, tablet, or desktop.
Can I manage several properties?
Yes — unlimited properties on one account, each with its own calendar and financials.
Does it connect to Airbnb automatically?
Currently you log reservations manually from any channel. Direct API sync is on the roadmap.
What does 'paid / unpaid' mean for direct bookings?
It tracks whether the guest has paid you, and whether you have paid your cleaner for that stay — two separate statuses.
Can my cleaning team use it too?
Yes — you can add team members who see their assigned cleanings and mark them done from their phone.
